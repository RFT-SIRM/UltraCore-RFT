Flagship engineering architecture · pre-experimental

RFT-QPU-Sierpinski

A proposed logical-qubit architecture built on a self-similar Sierpiński-gasket graph with non-commuting SU(2) edge transport. The level-1 design maps the graph to transmon modes and selects a two-dimensional low-energy subspace as a candidate logical qubit.

Stage 11B records a re-quantized three-mode A–coupler–B gate-model study. Its high projected overlap is a closed-system numerical result—not a measured gate fidelity or a full simulation of the 12-mode tile. No chip has been fabricated or measured.
