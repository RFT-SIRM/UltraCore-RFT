01 / Exact mathematical anchor

The fourth-moment trace defect

For the concrete non-commuting SU(2) connection and commuting control defined in the primary project, the identity holds for every refinement level and every real angle.

Lean 4 · formalizedm ≥ 1 · θ ∈ ℝ
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta_m^{(4)}(\theta) &=\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(H_C(m,\theta)^4\right) -\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(H_{C'}(m,\theta)^4\right)\\ &=-16\left(3^{m-1}+1\right) \sin^2\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right). \end{aligned} \]

Here the graph, edge orientations, SU(2) connection data, and commuting comparison operator are fixed by the primary theorem's definitions. The displayed formula is not a claim about an arbitrary graph or arbitrary connection.

\[ I_\infty(\theta) =-\frac{16}{9}\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right), \qquad I_\infty\!\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=-\frac89. \]

02 / Beyond the fourth moment

Higher moments — each with its own boundary

Do not flatten these results into a single “all moments proved” claim. The starting level and status of each finite transition matter.

Sixth moment · H⁶
Lean recurrence · m ≥ 3

Affine recurrence

\(D_{m+1}^{(6)}=3D_m^{(6)}+C_6\)

Lean proves the all-angle recurrence from level three. The exceptional transition from level two to three has an exact symbolic certificate, but is not yet a Lean-kernel theorem.

Scope: m ≥ 3 for the Lean recurrence; finite bridge kept separate.

Eighth moment · H⁸
Lean recurrence · m ≥ 3

Affine recurrence

\(D_{m+1}^{(8)}=3D_m^{(8)}+C_8\)

The recurrence is Lean-proved from level three. Its exceptional level-two-to-three transition is supported by an exact finite certificate with its own proof boundary.

Scope: m ≥ 3 for the Lean recurrence; finite bridge kept separate.

Tenth moment · H¹⁰
Lean recurrence · m ≥ 4

Radius-four transport

\(D_{m+1}^{(10)}=3D_m^{(10)}+C_{10}^{(5)}\)

Lean proves the radius-four transport and unconditional affine recurrence for level four onward. The affine closed form applies from level five; the level-five correction remains an exact finite trace expression.

Scope: recurrence m ≥ 4; affine form m ≥ 5.

Open extension: a unified general all-level recurrence for the broader higher-moment family remains open. See the primary higher-moment status record ↗.

Phi-Genesis: signed graph spectra

This is a separate $Z_2$-flux graph-matrix family, not the SU(2) connection family in the fourth-moment theorem. Its results have their own matrix definitions and level ranges.

What is proved — and what is not

Three proved components

Uniform bound

Every real eigenvalue of the explicitly defined signed Laplacian with a nonzero real eigenvector satisfies \(\lambda\ge1\), at every refinement level.

Least eigenvalue

For the signless matrices \(Q_N\) and their gauge-conjugate signed matrices, the canonical A1 Schur root is the least eigenvalue and the sequence converges for \(N\ge6\).

Derivative recursion

The two finite-level Schur boundary coordinates have Lean-proved parameter derivatives throughout the closed bootstrap interval. This is an analytic lemma, not the quadratic increment theorem.

04 / Systems methodology

SIRM: invariants belong to the runtime model

The SIRM equations describe constraints on state transitions. They are an independent engineering and mathematical strand, not a consequence of the spectral results above.

INVARIANT 01
S = B + Gp

Economic balance: total supply equals base balances plus the shared field contribution.

INVARIANT 02
S = M − D

Mint/burn ledger: total supply equals total minted minus total burned.

INVARIANT 03
d < p

Dust control, with participant count p positive.

INVARIANT 04
Eᵢ ≥ −S/(10p)

Debt ceiling: effective balance remains within the specified lower bound.

Effective balance: \(E_i=b_i+G\), under the model's documented integer and scaling conventions. Implementation claims are validated separately through code review, tests, fuzzing, and deployment evidence. See Foundations and Architecture.
