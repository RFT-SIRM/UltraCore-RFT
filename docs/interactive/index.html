01 / Mathematical anchor

One exact result. Clearly defined scope.

The source theorem concerns a specific SU(2)-connected operator on Sierpiński-gasket graphs and its commuting control.

Fourth-moment trace defect · source-stated identity
Δₘ(H⁴, θ) = Tr(H꜀⁴) − Tr(H꜀′⁴)
= −16(3ᵐ⁻¹ + 1) sin²(θ/2)

With the documented normalization, Iₘ(θ) = Δₘ / (3ᵐ⁺¹ + 3) tends to −(16/9) sin²(θ/2). At θ = π/2, the limiting value is exactly −8/9.

Scope boundary: the source reports a Lean 4 proof for its concrete formalized operators and stated quantifiers. It does not prove higher moments, a physical law, a quantum speed-up, SIRM runtime invariants, or a complete physical Theory of Everything. Independent review of the formal definitions remains a separate question.
